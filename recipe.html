This is a recipe for Lipi's special chai

The recipe for Lipi's special class includes:
    Ingredients:
  • Tea leaves
  • Sugar
  • Milk as you please
  • A teensi bit of water
The steps to make my amazing chai comprises of the steps mentioned below:-
  1. Boil teensi bit of water in the pot. When you start seeing bubbles, put tea leaves & sugar in it and then let it come to a good boil.
  2. During the boil, grate ginger in it. Personally, I also like some basil leaves in my tea, but if you don't like it, then you can avoid it.
  3. After the boil repeats itself for 3-4 times, pour milk into it.
  4. Now comes the part where you feel like a true tapri wala since your chai will show a boil just like theirs do.
  5. One of my most fav part of making chai is to let it boil until it comes up to the brim, and then lift the utensil, swirl it round and round 1-2 times and then put it back on the stove. It truly makes chai very tasty, trust me.
  6. After that, I let it boil in a low flame. After good 3-4 minutes, turn the stove off, pour the chai into the cup, and then savour its taste
